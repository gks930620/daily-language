소비·기술 — 돈으로 사고파는 별점, 가짜 온라인 리뷰 산업과 무너지는 '집단 지성'의 신뢰(해설 기사)

For most shoppers, a row of glowing five-star reviews has become the modern equivalent of word of mouth, the reassurance that a stranger has already tested a product and is willing to vouch for it. Yet an entire clandestine industry now exists to manufacture that reassurance: brokers recruit reviewers through private chat groups, offering full refunds or small kickbacks in exchange for praise of items they have barely used. Platforms insist they remove millions of bogus posts each year, but critics counter that the companies have little reason to look harder, since a page plastered with positive ratings tends to turn browsers into buyers. Regulators have begun to respond, with new rules that allow fines for businesses that buy fake testimonials or suppress negative ones, though enforcement remains patchy because the sellers are often small, anonymous firms based overseas. The deeper cost may be cultural rather than financial: once consumers learn that a perfect score can be bought, even honest reviews are tarnished, and the collective wisdom that ratings promised gives way to reflexive suspicion.

💡 명사 '역병(plague)'이 동사로 — 병이 들러붙듯 문제가 끈질기게 괴롭힌다. 주로 수동태 be plagued by/with ~로 쓴다.
be plagued by problems, plague the industry

lopsided a. 한쪽으로 치우친, 일방적인

The negotiations produced a lopsided deal that heavily favored the larger company.
협상 결과 큰 회사에 크게 유리한 일방적인 합의가 나왔다.

💡 lop(축 처지다)+side — 한쪽이 처져 기울어진 모양. 스포츠 뉴스의 a lopsided win(일방적 승리)으로 자주 보인다.
a lopsided victory, a lopsided distribution

mar v. 망치다, 흠집 내다

Technical glitches marred the otherwise successful product launch.
기술적 결함이 그것만 아니면 성공적이었을 제품 출시를 망쳤다.

💡 수동태 be marred by(~로 얼룩지다)가 기사 빈출. 활용은 자음을 겹쳐 marred·marring.
구분tarnish(더럽히다) — mar는 행사·경험·외관을 망치는 것, tarnish는 명성·이미지를 더럽히는 것
be marred by, mar the occasion
