오늘의 문단

교육·보건 — 스마트폰과 청소년 정신건강, 학교 전면 금지를 둘러싼 논쟁(논설)

Few contemporary claims have travelled as quickly from anecdote to conventional wisdom as the assertion that smartphones are responsible for a collapse in adolescent mental health. The evidence is more equivocal than the headlines suggest: rates of anxiety and self-harm did climb steeply after 2012, yet longitudinal studies that follow individual teenagers over time find associations too small, and too easily confounded by family circumstance, to establish causation. Skeptics are right to insist that correlation is not proof, and that every generation has attributed its discontents to whatever technology happened to be new. Yet the precautionary case for acting does not rest on certainty: sleep deprivation and the sheer opportunity cost of six hours a day are harms that require no elaborate theory of causation, and schools that have imposed outright bans during the day report calmer corridors and steadier attention. The danger is that a prohibition imposed without explanation will backfire, driving use underground and leaving adolescents no better equipped to moderate a device they will carry for the rest of their lives.

장관은 그 세금에 대한 분명한 입장을 또렷이 밝히는 데 애를 먹었다.

💡 라틴어 articulus(마디)에서 왔다 — 생각을 마디마디 끊어 또렷하게 내놓는다. article(기사·조항)도 '마디로 나뉜 것'이라 같은 뿌리다. 동사는 끝을 [-에이트], 형용사는 [-엇]으로 발음이 갈린다.
파생articulation(n.) 명확한 표현; 발음
articulate a vision, clearly articulated
