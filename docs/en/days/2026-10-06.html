오늘의 문단

사회·교육 — 모두가 A를 받는 대학, '성적 인플레이션'이 결국 우수한 학생에게 불리해지는 역설(논설)

Over the past three decades, the average grade awarded at many universities has crept steadily upward, to the point where an A — once reserved for genuinely exceptional work — has become the single most common mark on a transcript. Defenders argue that today's students are simply better prepared, but most researchers attribute the trend to subtler pressures: instructors who rely on student evaluations for promotion have little incentive to antagonize the very people grading them in return. Had grades merely drifted upward in step with real gains in learning, few would object; the trouble is that when nearly everyone receives top marks, the scale is compressed at the top and loses its power to differentiate. Employers and graduate schools, unable to tell a merely competent applicant from an outstanding one, increasingly turn to other signals, such as internships, standardized tests, or the prestige of the institution itself. The irony, critics note, is that a practice meant to spare students disappointment may end up penalizing the most capable among them, whose achievements are now indistinguishable from those of their peers.

  5. The irony, critics note, is that a practice meant to spare students disappointment may end up penalizing the most capable among them, whose achievements are now indistinguishable from those of their peers.

비판자들은 그 방송사가 시청률을 위해 시청자의 저급한 본능에 영합한다고 비난했다.

💡 초서의 이야기 속 남녀를 이어 주던 인물 Pandarus에서 — '남의 욕망을 채워 주는 사람'. 그래서 늘 pander to + 대상 형태로, '원칙 없이 비위만 맞춘다'는 비판조로 쓴다.
구분cater to((요구를) 충족시키다) — cater to는 중립적 '맞춰 주다'(cater to families), pander to는 부정적 '영합하다'
pander to, pander to voters

attest v. 증명하다, 입증하다

Long lines outside the store attest to the brand's enduring popularity.
가게 밖의 긴 줄은 그 브랜드의 변함없는 인기를 입증한다.

💡 test-(증인)에서 — testify(증언하다), testimony(증언), contest(함께 증인 세움=겨루다), protest(앞에서 증언=항의하다)와 한 뿌리. attest to + 명사로 '~을 보여 주다'.
파생attestation(n.) 증명, 증명서
구분testify(증언하다) — testify는 주로 법정 증언, attest는 사물이 주어로 와 '~을 증명해 준다'는 쓰임이 많다
attest to, as many can attest

lament v./n. 한탄하다; 비탄

Many small-business owners lament the loss of foot traffic downtown.
많은 소상공인들이 도심 유동 인구의 감소를 한탄한다.

💡 파생에서 뜻이 갈라지는 단어: lamentable은 '한탄할 만한'에서 '한심한, 형편없는'으로 흘렀다(a lamentable performance = 형편없는 공연).
파생lamentable(a.) 한탄스러운; 형편없는lamentation(n.) 비탄, 애도
lament the loss of, widely lamented
