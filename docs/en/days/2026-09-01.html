해운·기후 — 가뭄이 막아 세운 운하와 세계 물류의 병목(논설)

For more than a century the canal has functioned as a chokepoint through which a substantial share of world trade passes, its locks lifting laden vessels over the isthmus on water drawn from a single rain-fed reservoir. That arrangement, long taken for granted, has proved unexpectedly fragile: a succession of drought years lowered the reservoir to the point that the authority was compelled to ration transits, auctioning off a dwindling number of slots to the highest bidder. Carriers that declined to bid were left to queue for weeks or to reroute around the southern tip of the continent, a detour that adds thousands of nautical miles and burns fuel accordingly. The resulting freight surcharges rippled outward to consumers who had never heard of the waterway, a reminder that logistics remains invisible until it fails. Dredging deeper channels and building new reservoirs would buy time, but engineers concede that no amount of concrete can compensate for rainfall that no longer arrives on schedule.

💡 ardu-(가파른)에서 온 말이라 가파른 비탈을 오르듯 '오래 힘든' 일에 붙는다 — task·journey·process처럼 긴 과정과 어울리고, 잠깐 어려운 일에는 잘 쓰지 않는다.
구분grueling(녹초로 만드는) — grueling은 체력이 바닥나는 소모전, arduous는 끈기가 필요한 험한 과정 — 둘 다 '고된'으로 번역되지만 초점이 다르다
arduous task, arduous journey, arduous process

vigilant a. 경계를 늦추지 않는, 방심하지 않는

Regulators promised to remain vigilant against price manipulation.
규제 당국은 가격 조작에 대해 계속 경계를 늦추지 않겠다고 약속했다.

💡 vigil(밤샘 감시)과 한 뿌리로 '자지 않고 지켜본다'는 뜻이다. wary가 의심스러워 조심하는 소극적 태도라면 vigilant는 위험을 놓치지 않으려 계속 지켜보는 능동적 감시다.
파생vigilance(n.) 경계, 조심vigilante(n.) 자경단원 — '경계하는 사람'이 아니라 법 절차를 무시하고 직접 처벌에 나서는 사람
remain vigilant, vigilant against, ever vigilant
