오늘의 문단

경제·조세 — 다국적기업 최저한세와 이익 이전(profit shifting)을 둘러싼 논쟁(논설)

For decades, multinational corporations have exploited the mismatch between where profits are booked and where value is actually created, routing revenue through subsidiaries in jurisdictions that levy little or no corporate tax. The global minimum tax, endorsed by more than 130 countries, was meant to put a floor under this race to the bottom: whenever profits are taxed abroad at less than fifteen percent, a parent company's home government may collect a top-up levy. Yet the agreement is riddled with carve-outs that were the price of consensus, and several signatories have quietly deferred implementation while waiting to see whether their competitors do the same. Critics contend that the deal entrenches the advantage of large economies: developing countries, whose revenues depend far more heavily on corporate taxation, cede their right to tax income at its source in return for a share of revenue that may never materialize. Whether the regime holds will depend less on the elegance of its drafting than on whether any government is willing to forfeit an investment it could have lured with a tax break.

💡 nasc-/nat-(태어나다) 뿌리로 nature·native·prenatal과 한 계열. new와 달리 '갓 태어나 아직 약한'이라는 취약함이 함께 실려서, nascent industry는 보호 논거로 쓰이는 표현이다.

espouse v. (주장·정책을) 지지하다, 신봉하다

The minister espoused free trade in public while defending subsidies at home.
그 장관은 공개적으로는 자유무역을 지지하면서 국내에서는 보조금을 옹호했다.

💡 spous-(약속하다) 뿌리로 spouse(배우자)와 한 집안 — '주장과 혼인하듯 결합하다'가 '신봉하다'가 됐다. 목적어는 사람이 아니라 늘 사상·명분·정책이다.
구분endorse(지지하다, 보증하다) — endorse는 사람·상품도 공개 지지할 수 있지만, espouse는 사상·명분에만 붙는다
espouse a cause, espouse a view
