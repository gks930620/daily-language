오늘의 문단

사회·문화 — 약탈 문화재 반환을 둘러싼 박물관 논쟁(논설)

For more than a century, the great museums of Europe have justified their possession of looted antiquities by casting themselves as disinterested custodians of a common human heritage. That argument, once accepted almost without question, has come under sustained attack from source countries that regard the objects less as universal treasures than as tangible fragments of their own severed histories. Defenders of the status quo warn that any concession would open the floodgates, stripping encyclopedic collections of the very works that draw millions of visitors each year. Critics counter that such fears are overstated, noting that the restitutions carried out so far have neither emptied a single gallery nor diminished public interest in the institutions that made them. What has shifted, in the end, is not the law — which remains stubbornly favorable to the holders — but the moral vocabulary in which the dispute is conducted, and museums that ignore that shift may find their legitimacy eroding long before any court compels them to act.

  5. What has shifted, in the end, is not the law — which remains stubbornly favorable to the holders — but the moral vocabulary in which the dispute is conducted, and museums that ignore that shift may find their legitimacy eroding long before any court compels them to act.

    해석 · 단어 · 구문 분석

    결국 달라진 것은 법이 아니라 — 법은 여전히 완강하게 소유자 쪽에 유리하다 — 그 논쟁이 이루어지는 도덕적 언어이며, 그 변화를 무시하는 박물관들은 어떤 법원이 행동을 강제하기 훨씬 전에 자신들의 정당성이 무너져 내리고 있음을 알게 될지도 모른다.

    단어

    • shift — 바뀌다; 변화

    • holder — 소유자, 보유자
    • vocabulary — 어휘, (특정 분야의) 용어

    • conduct — 수행하다, (논의를) 진행하다

    • erode — 서서히 무너뜨리다, 침식하다

    구문 분석

    · What has shifted ~ is not A but B: 관계대명사 what이 이끄는 명사절이 주어, 「not A but B」가 보어. · which remains ~ : 계속적 용법의 관계대명사(선행사 the law). 삽입된 설명이라 대시로 묶였다. · in which the dispute is conducted: 「전치사 + 관계대명사」, 선행사는 the moral vocabulary(= the vocabulary in it). · find their legitimacy eroding: 「find + 목적어 + 현재분사」 '~이 ~하고 있음을 알게 되다'. · long before ~ : '~하기 훨씬 전에'. long이 before절 전체를 강조한다.
