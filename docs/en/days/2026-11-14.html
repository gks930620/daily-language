사회 — 외로움을 '처방'으로 다스리는 사회적 처방의 가능성과 한계(기사)

When Britain appointed its first minister for loneliness in 2018, many commentators dismissed the move as a gimmick, yet the evidence that has accumulated since suggests that prolonged isolation is far more than an emotional inconvenience. Researchers now link chronic loneliness to a higher risk of heart disease, dementia and early death, with some estimates likening its health toll to that of smoking fifteen cigarettes a day. In response, a growing number of health systems have begun experimenting with what is known as social prescribing, in which doctors refer patients not to a specialist but to community activities such as gardening clubs, choirs or volunteer groups. Advocates argue that such programs are cost-effective precisely because they target the underlying causes of ill health rather than merely treating its symptoms, thereby easing pressure on overstretched clinics. Critics, however, caution that the evidence remains patchy, and that without sustained funding for the very community groups to which patients are referred, prescribing companionship may prove to be little more than a well-meaning gesture.

The firm has been embroiled in a lengthy legal battle with its former partner.
그 회사는 이전 파트너와의 긴 법정 다툼에 휘말려 있다.

💡 주로 수동태 be embroiled in ~(~에 휘말리다)로 쓴다. em-(안으로) + broil(혼란·소동) — 소동 '안으로' 끌려 들어간 그림.
구분embroider(수를 놓다; (이야기를) 과장하다) — 철자 혼동어 — 뜻은 전혀 다르다entangle(얽히게 하다) — 비슷한 뜻 — entangle은 물리적·복잡한 관계에, embroil은 분쟁·스캔들에 잘 붙는다
be embroiled in a dispute, embroiled in scandal

headway n. 진전, 전진

Negotiators made little headway in the first round of talks.
협상단은 첫 회담에서 거의 진전을 보지 못했다.

💡 거의 항상 make headway (in/on/against) 형태. 원래 배가 '앞(head)으로 나아가는 길(way)'에서 왔다 — 역풍 headwind와 한 글자 차이지만 뜻은 정반대 방향.
구분headwind(역풍, 악재) — head- 혼동어 — headwind는 앞을 막는 바람, headway는 앞으로 나아감
make headway, make little headway

deluge n. 폭우, 홍수; (편지·문의 등의) 쇄도

The help desk received a deluge of complaints after the update.
업데이트 이후 고객 센터에 불만이 쇄도했다.

💡 de-(아래로) + luere(씻다) — 원래 '쏟아지는 큰물'. 비즈니스·뉴스에선 'a deluge of + 복수명사'로 '감당 못 할 만큼 몰려드는 것'을 비유적으로 쓴다(be deluged with = ~이 쇄도하다).
구분flood(홍수; 쇄도) — 같은 비유로 쓰지만 deluge가 더 격식 있고 규모가 압도적인 느낌
a deluge of complaints, a deluge of applications
