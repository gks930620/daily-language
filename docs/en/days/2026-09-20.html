기술·에너지 — 데이터센터의 전력·냉각수 수요와 그 비용을 떠안는 지역사회(논설)

Few industries have grown as quietly, or as voraciously, as the data centers that now anchor the digital economy, and the bill for their appetite is arriving in places that never agreed to pay it. Utilities that once forecast flat demand are suddenly fielding interconnection requests large enough to rival a mid-sized city, and the transmission lines and baseload capacity needed to serve them will be financed, in the end, by ratepayers. Local officials, courted with promises of investment and generous tax abatements, have often approved siting decisions before the water footprint of evaporative cooling was ever disclosed. The resulting pushback has been sharpest in drought-prone counties, where residents face restrictions on their own consumption while a windfall of new construction proceeds unimpeded. Defenders counter that the objection is not germane: the same computation performed elsewhere would consume energy anyway, and often on a dirtier grid — an argument that is plausible in the aggregate and cold comfort to the county whose aquifer is being drawn down.

germane a. (논점과) 밀접한 관련이 있는

That objection is not germane to the question before the committee.
그 반론은 위원회가 다루는 사안과는 관련이 없다.

💡 relevant보다 한 단계 격식 있는 말로 거의 항상 be germane to 형태로 쓴다. 나라 이름 German과는 무관하고, 원래 뜻은 '같은 부모에게서 난(가까운)'이다.
구분relevant(관련 있는) — 뜻은 거의 같지만 germane은 회의·논증·법정처럼 격식 있는 맥락에 주로 쓰인다
germane to the discussion, be germane to ~

inordinate a. 과도한, 지나친

Small firms spend an inordinate amount of time on compliance paperwork.
소규모 기업들은 규정 준수 서류 작업에 지나치게 많은 시간을 쓴다.

💡 in-(아닌) + ordinate(질서가 잡힌) = '정해진 정도를 벗어난'. order 계열(ordinary·coordinate)에 속하며, 특히 amount·number·delay 같은 양·시간 명사를 꾸민다.
파생inordinately(ad.) 지나치게
an inordinate amount of time, inordinate delay
