과학·보건 — 피·폐·뇌에서까지 미세플라스틱이 나왔다? 공포스러운 헤드라인과 그 뒤에 숨은 측정의 불확실성(논설)

Few environmental stories have spread as quickly as the claim that microplastics have infiltrated nearly every corner of the human body, from blood and lungs to placentas and even brain tissue. The headlines are understandably alarming, yet a growing number of chemists caution that the science behind them is far murkier than the coverage suggests. Because plastic particles are ubiquitous in laboratory air, gloves and equipment, samples can easily become contaminated, and some widely cited studies have been criticized for failing to rule out such interference. Detection methods, moreover, remain so inconsistent that estimates of how much plastic a person ingests each week vary by several orders of magnitude, making it nearly impossible to gauge the actual risk. None of this means the concern is unfounded; rather, it suggests that regulators would be better served by standardizing measurement than by reacting to every startling figure that makes the news.

그 광고는 휴대전화의 배터리 수명을 크게 과장했다.

💡 철자 함정: g가 두 개(exa-gg-erate), r은 하나. 라틴어 agger(쌓아 올린 더미)에서 — 사실 위에 흙을 '쌓아 올려' 크게 보이게 하다.
파생exaggeration(n.) 과장exaggerated(a.) 과장된
구분overstate(과장하다) — overstate는 수치·정도를 실제보다 크게 '말하는' 것, exaggerate는 말·행동 전반의 과장
