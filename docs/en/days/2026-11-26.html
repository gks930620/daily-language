심리학 — 우리는 왜 감사 표현의 힘을 과소평가하는가(교양 기사)

Psychologists have long known that expressing gratitude boosts the well-being of both the giver and the recipient, but most people rarely bother to write a thank-you note. A series of experiments conducted by American researchers suggests one reason why: we systematically underestimate how deeply our appreciation will resonate with others. Participants who wrote heartfelt letters predicted that the recipients would feel only mildly pleased and even a little awkward, but the recipients reported being far happier than the writers had anticipated. The writers, it turned out, had fixated on whether their words sounded articulate enough, overlooking the fact that recipients cared mostly about the warmth behind the gesture. Had they perceived this gap more accurately, the researchers argue, many would not have hesitated to reap the benefits of such a simple, cost-free act.
