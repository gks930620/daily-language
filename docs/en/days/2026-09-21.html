Few industries have mastered the art of manufactured urgency as thoroughly as fast fashion, which now releases new collections not four times a year but nearly every week. The garments are priced so low that consumers treat them as effectively disposable, and roughly three in five are discarded within a year of purchase. What is billed as charitable donation frequently turns out to be an export business: bales of unsold and unwanted clothing are shipped to markets in West Africa and South Asia, where traders buy them sight unseen and absorb whatever cannot be resold. Because a growing share of these consignments consists of synthetic fibers that will neither biodegrade nor burn cleanly, the surplus ends up in open dumps and waterways, and the cost of disposal falls on municipalities that never profited from the sale. 💡 discretion(재량; 신중함)의 형용사. 예산 기사의 discretionary spending은 '법으로 고정돼 있지 않아 해마다 깎을 수 있는 지출'로, mandatory spending(의무지출)과 늘 짝으로 나온다.
파생discretion(n.) 재량; 신중함discreet(a.) 신중한, 입이 무거운 — 철자가 비슷한 discrete(별개의)와 다름
discretionary spending, discretionary bonus

repercussion n. (좋지 않은) 파급 영향, 후폭풍

The export ban had repercussions far beyond the industry it was meant to protect.
그 수출 금지 조치는 보호하려던 산업을 훨씬 넘어서는 파급 영향을 낳았다.

💡 re-(되)+percussion(두드림) — 친 것이 '되돌아와 울리는' 것. 대개 복수형으로 쓰고, 좋은 결과에는 붙이지 않는 부정적인 말이다.
구분ramification(파생 결과) — ramification은 가지처럼 뻗어 나가는 결과라 중립적, repercussion은 되돌아와 때리는 악영향
serious repercussions, political repercussions
