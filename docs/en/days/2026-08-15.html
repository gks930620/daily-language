오늘의 문단

환경·재난 — 전면 진압 정책이 키운 산불과 '계획 소각'을 둘러싼 논쟁(논설)

For most of the past century, fire agencies across the American West adhered to a doctrine of total suppression, extinguishing every blaze before it could spread. That policy, prudent as it seemed, allowed undergrowth and dead timber to accumulate into a vast reservoir of tinder that no ordinary summer could burn off. Ecologists now argue that the only remedy is to reintroduce fire deliberately — prescribed burns, set under damp and windless conditions, that consume flammable debris without threatening nearby dwellings. Yet the practice remains politically fraught: a single escaped ember can incinerate the homes it was meant to protect, and the agency that lit the fire, rather than the decades of neglect that preceded it, will be blamed in the aftermath. Until residents accept that a smoky spring is the price of surviving an incendiary autumn, the West will keep paying for containment it can no longer afford.

송전선에서 튄 불꽃 하나가 몇 주 동안 이어진 화재를 일으켰다.

The fire had reached 60 percent containment by Friday morning.

All contractors must adhere to the safety guidelines on site.

💡 ad-(~에) + here(붙다) — 물리적으로 '붙다'에서 '규칙에 딱 붙어 지키다'로 넓어졌다. 자동사라 목적어 앞에 to가 반드시 오고(adhere to the rules), adhesive(접착제)가 같은 뿌리다.
파생adherence(n.) 준수, 고수adhesive(n.) 접착제
adhere to guidelines, adhere to a schedule

incur v. (비용·손실·비난을) 초래하다, 떠안다

The company incurred heavy losses during the downturn.
그 회사는 경기 침체기에 큰 손실을 입었다.

💡 in-(안으로) + cur(달리다) = '내 쪽으로 달려 들어오게 하다'. 좋은 일에는 안 쓰고 cost·debt·loss·criticism·penalty처럼 나쁜 것만 목적어로 받는다. cur(달리다) 집안: occur, recur, current.
구분occur(일어나다, 발생하다) — 철자가 비슷해 헷갈리지만 incur는 '내가 떠안다'(타동사), occur는 '저절로 일어나다'(자동사)로 주어가 다르다
incur costs, incur a penalty
