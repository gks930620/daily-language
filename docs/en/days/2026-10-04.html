오늘의 문단

환경·자원 — 가뭄의 해법으로 떠오른 해수 담수화와 그 뒤에 남는 소금물·에너지 비용(논설)

As droughts grow longer and aquifers are pumped dry, a growing number of arid nations are turning to the sea, hailing desalination as the answer to a problem that once seemed intractable. The technology itself is no longer in doubt: modern plants push seawater through fine membranes at high pressure, stripping out salt and producing water clean enough to quench the thirst of entire cities. Yet every litre of fresh water leaves behind roughly one and a half litres of brine, which is usually pumped back into the sea, where its dense, salty plumes can sink to the seabed and smother life there. The energy bill is no less sobering, since forcing water through membranes consumes vast amounts of electricity, much of which is still generated by burning fossil fuels, the very source of the warming that worsens drought. None of this means the plants should never be built, but it does suggest that the cheaper, duller remedies—repairing leaky pipes, recycling wastewater, and pricing water so that it is not squandered—deserve to come first.
