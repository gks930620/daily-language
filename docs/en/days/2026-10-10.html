과학·식품 — 공장이 아닌 배양 탱크에서 키운 '배양육'은 왜 기술보다 소비자의 거부감이라는 벽에 더 오래 막혀 있는가(논설)

Few food technologies have been touted as loudly, or met with as much skepticism, as meat cultivated from animal cells in steel tanks rather than raised on farms. Proponents argue that growing muscle tissue in sterile bioreactors could one day spare billions of livestock from slaughter while slashing the land, water and emissions that conventional farming demands. Yet more than a decade after the first lab-grown burger was unveiled at a staggering cost of over $300,000, the industry still faces daunting hurdles: production remains difficult to scale up, and price parity with ordinary beef looks years away. Even if the technical problems are solved, consumer aversion may prove harder to overcome, since many shoppers are squeamish about food that sounds as if it came out of a laboratory rather than a pasture. Whether the product makes real inroads will depend less on what scientists can engineer than on whether companies can persuade the public that "lab-grown meat" is a misnomer for something that is, ultimately, still meat.

The company released safety data to allay consumers' concerns.
회사는 소비자의 우려를 가라앉히기 위해 안전성 자료를 공개했다.

💡 목적어가 거의 fears·concerns·doubts(감정·우려)로 정해져 있다. alleviate는 고통·부담, mitigate는 위험·피해에 붙는 것과 구별 — '우려를 덜다'는 allay가 가장 자연스럽다.
구분alley(골목) — 발음이 같은 혼동어(앨리) — 철자 a-ll-a-y vs a-ll-e-y
allay fears, allay concerns, allay suspicion

dissuade v. (설득해서) 단념시키다

High fees may dissuade small businesses from joining the platform.
높은 수수료는 소상공인들이 그 플랫폼에 가입하는 것을 단념시킬 수 있다.

💡 dis-(반대로) + suade(권하다) = persuade(설득하다)의 반대. persuade A to do ↔ dissuade A from -ing — 뒤에 오는 형태가 to부정사/from 동명사로 갈리는 것까지 세트로 외우자.
구분persuade(설득하다) — 반대말 — persuade A to do(하도록 설득) vs dissuade A from doing(하지 않도록 설득)deter(단념시키다, 억제하다) — deter는 두려움·불이익으로 막는 것, dissuade는 말로 설득해 그만두게 하는 것
dissuade A from -ing
