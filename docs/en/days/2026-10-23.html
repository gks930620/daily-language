과학·건강 — 장내 미생물(마이크로바이옴) 연구와 프로바이오틱 열풍에 대한 경고(해설 기사)

For decades, the trillions of bacteria residing in the human gut were regarded as little more than passive passengers, tolerated but largely irrelevant to our health. That view has been upended by a growing body of research suggesting that these microbes help regulate everything from digestion and immunity to mood, and that their composition is shaped far more by diet than by genetics. In one widely cited study, volunteers who ate fermented foods such as yogurt and kimchi for ten weeks showed a marked increase in microbial diversity, along with reduced markers of inflammation. Such findings have fueled a booming market for probiotic supplements, many of which promise benefits that their manufacturers have yet to substantiate in rigorous trials. Scientists caution that the microbiome is highly individual, meaning that a regimen which restores balance in one person may do little, or even cause harm, in another.

caution v./n. 경고하다, 주의를 주다 / 조심, 주의

Analysts caution that the recovery may be slower than the government expects.

salutary a. (불쾌하지만) 유익한, 교훈이 되는

The failed product launch was a salutary lesson for the entire marketing team.
제품 출시 실패는 마케팅팀 전체에 쓰지만 유익한 교훈이 되었다.
