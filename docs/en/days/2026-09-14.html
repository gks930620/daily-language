우주·규제 — 저궤도 위성 급증과 우주 쓰레기, 누가 치울 것인가(논설)

Barely a decade ago, the notion that low Earth orbit could become crowded was dismissed as an astronomer's preoccupation; today more than ten thousand active satellites traverse that narrow band. The commercial case is compelling — broadband beamed to regions that terrestrial networks never found lucrative — but each new constellation brings the orbit closer to the threshold at which one collision could spawn a cascade of debris. Regulators have been left to delineate rules for a commons no single state governs, and the incentives cut the wrong way: an operator that deorbits a defunct satellite bears the cost alone, while the clearer sky accrues to every rival it vies with. Some proposals would require operators to post a bond forfeited if a payload is abandoned at altitude; others would have launching states cede jurisdiction to a body empowered to impose binding deadlines. Yet the physics is inexorable: the fragments already aloft will not vanish because a treaty is signed, and every year of delay leaves astronomers, whose long exposures are streaked by passing satellites, with less to salvage.

구분secede(탈퇴하다, 분리 독립하다) — se-(따로) + cede(가다) — 조직에서 '따로 떨어져 나가다'
cede control, cede territory, cede ground

inexorable a. 막을 수 없는, 냉혹한

The inexorable rise in maintenance costs finally forced the plant to close.
막을 수 없는 유지비 상승이 결국 그 공장을 문 닫게 만들었다.

💡 in-(부정) + exorare(간청해서 마음을 움직이다) = '아무리 빌어도 꺾이지 않는'. 그래서 사람보다 추세·법칙·논리처럼 사정을 봐주지 않는 흐름에 붙는다.
구분relentless(수그러들지 않는, 집요한) — relentless는 사람의 집요함에도 쓰지만 inexorable은 주로 비인격적 흐름에 쓴다
inexorable rise, inexorable logic, inexorable decline

delineate v. (경계를) 긋다, 명확히 규정하다

The contract clearly delineates the responsibilities of each party.
그 계약서는 각 당사자의 책임을 명확히 규정하고 있다.

💡 de- + line(선) = '선을 그어 내다'. 지도의 경계선 같은 물리적 구획과 역할·책임 같은 추상적 구분 양쪽에 쓴다.
파생delineation(n.) 구획, 명확한 규정
구분define(정의하다) — define은 '무엇인지'를 말해 주고, delineate는 '어디까지인지' 경계를 그어 준다
clearly delineate, delineate boundaries, delineate roles
