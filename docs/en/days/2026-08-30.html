형사사법 — 현금 보석(bail) 제도 폐지 논쟁과 재범을 둘러싼 공방(논설)

For decades, courts across much of the country have set bail as a matter of routine, requiring defendants to post cash they often cannot raise in order to avoid detention before trial. Reformers contend that the practice punishes poverty rather than dangerousness, since an indigent defendant charged with a minor offense may languish in jail for months while a wealthier one accused of a violent felony walks free within hours. Several jurisdictions have accordingly curtailed cash bail, instructing magistrates to release most defendants outright and to remand only those deemed genuinely likely to abscond or reoffend. Prosecutors and police unions have responded with an outcry, pointing to a handful of widely publicized crimes committed by people out on release, though the statistical case they make remains tenuous. Early evaluations tally little change in recidivism and a sharp drop in pretrial incarceration, but the ultimate verdict may hinge less on the data than on whether the public can be persuaded that a crowded docket and an empty jail are not, in themselves, a failure of justice.

구분tenacious(집요한, 끈질긴) — tenuous(가늘다)와 달리 ten-(붙잡다) 계열 — 뜻이 오히려 정반대에 가깝다
a tenuous link, a tenuous grip, tenuous evidence

hinge v. (~에) 달려 있다 (hinge on)

The deal hinges on whether the regulator approves the merger.
그 거래의 성사는 규제 당국이 합병을 승인하느냐에 달려 있다.

💡 명사로는 '경첩'이다 — 문 전체가 경첩 하나에 매달려 돌아가듯 '전부가 그것 하나에 달려 있다'는 뜻이 나왔다. depend on보다 '그것이 결정적'이라는 어감이 훨씬 강하다.
구분contingent(~에 달려 있는, 조건부의) — be contingent on도 같은 뜻이지만 계약서 같은 격식 문서에 더 잘 어울린다
hinge on the outcome, everything hinges on

vet v. (사람·자료를) 사전 심사하다, 검증하다

All applicants are carefully vetted before they are offered a contract.
모든 지원자는 계약을 제안받기 전에 꼼꼼히 신원 검증을 거친다.

💡 veterinarian(수의사)에서 온 말로, 경주마를 출전 전에 수의사가 검사하던 데서 '미리 걸러 본다'는 뜻이 되었다. 활용할 때 자음을 겹쳐 vetted/vetting으로 쓰는 점에 주의.
파생vetting(n.) 사전 심사, 신원 조회
vet candidates, thoroughly vetted, the vetting process
