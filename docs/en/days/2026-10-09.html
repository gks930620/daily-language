오늘의 문단

과학·기술 — 점토판은 수천 년을 버티는데 오늘의 파일은 한 세대도 못 간다? '디지털 암흑시대'와 데이터 보존의 역설(논설)

Historians have long relied on clay tablets and parchment that survived for millennia, yet many of the digital records we produce today may not outlast a single generation. Files saved in obsolete formats can become unreadable once the software needed to open them is discontinued, and storage media such as hard drives quietly degrade even when left untouched. Some archivists warn of a coming "digital dark age," in which future scholars will find it harder to decipher an email from 2005 than a letter written in 1805. To keep data intact, libraries now copy files to new systems every few years and even build programs that emulate outdated computers, but such preservation is costly and never truly finished. Ironically, the very ease with which we can create and delete information may be what consigns much of our era to oblivion, unless we decide, deliberately and early, what is worth saving for posterity.

facilitate v. 용이하게 하다, 촉진하다

The new software is designed to facilitate communication between remote teams.
새 소프트웨어는 원격 팀 간의 소통을 원활하게 하도록 설계되었다.

💡 facile(쉬운) + -ate(만들다) — '쉽게 만들다'. 주의: 사람을 목적어로 쓰지 않는다(×facilitate him) — 일·과정을 목적어로 쓴다.
파생facilitator(n.) 진행자, 조력자facility(n.) 시설; (하는) 재능 — '쉽게 해 주는 것'에서 '시설'로 갈라짐
facilitate communication, facilitate trade
