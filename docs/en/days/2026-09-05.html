사회·규제 — 스포츠 베팅 합법화가 남긴 사회적 비용과 뒤늦은 단속(논설)

Barely a decade after the first states legalized it, sports betting has migrated from back-alley bookmaking to the glowing screens that hover above every stadium concourse. Proponents insisted that bringing the market into the open would starve criminal operators and channel a steady stream of tax proceeds into public coffers. What they did not advertise was that the same platforms would bankroll saturation advertising, and that odds would be pushed at viewers in real time, second by second, until placing a wager felt indistinguishable from watching the game itself. Problem gambling, once dismissed as a fringe affliction, is now rampant among young men whose losses have quietly become ruinous, and clinicians warn that the frictionless design of these apps is precisely what makes them addictive. Regulators, meanwhile, have been reluctant to sanction the very industry whose revenues they have come to depend on, and a crackdown that arrives only after the harm has been counted is no substitute for one that anticipates it.

  1. Barely a decade after the first states legalized it, sports betting has migrated from back-alley bookmaking to the glowing screens that hover above every stadium concourse.

💡 ambi-(양쪽)+val(가치) — 두 가지 감정이 동시에 있어 결정을 못 하는 상태다. indifferent(무관심한)와 정반대에 가깝다: ambivalent는 둘 다 강하게 느껴서 못 고르는 것.
