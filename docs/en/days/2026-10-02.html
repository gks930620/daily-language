사회·보건 — 아침잠 많은 십대는 게으른 걸까, 학교 등교 시간 늦추기 개혁과 그 걸림돌(논설)

For decades, teenagers who struggled to get out of bed before dawn were dismissed as lazy, but a growing body of research suggests that their grogginess is largely biological rather than a failing of character. During adolescence, the body's internal clock shifts by as much as two hours, so that a sixteen-year-old forced to wake at six is, in effect, being asked to function in the middle of his or her own night. Districts that have pushed back their first bell to 8:30 or later report modest but consistent gains: attendance improves, tardiness declines, and car accidents involving young drivers fall noticeably. Yet the reform has proved surprisingly hard to sell, because a later start ripples through bus schedules, after-school sports, and the working hours of parents who rely on dropping their children off on the way to the office. Critics also warn that teenagers may simply stay up later to compensate, although studies tracking students over several years have found that most of the extra time in the morning is genuinely spent asleep.

  1. For decades, teenagers who struggled to get out of bed before dawn were dismissed as lazy, but a growing body of research suggests that their grogginess is largely biological rather than a failing of character.

    해석 · 단어 · 구문 분석

    수십 년 동안 동트기 전에 침대에서 일어나느라 애를 먹는 십대들은 게으르다고 치부되어 왔지만, 점점 늘어나는 연구들은 그들의 몽롱함이 성격상의 결함이라기보다 대체로 생물학적인 것임을 시사한다.

    단어

    • for decades — 수십 년 동안

    • dawn — 새벽, 동틀 녘
    • dismiss as — ~라고 치부하다, 일축하다
    • lazy — 게으른
    • a growing body of research — 점점 늘어나는 연구들
    • suggest — 시사하다, 암시하다
    • grogginess — (잠이 덜 깨) 몽롱함, 비몽사몽
    • largely — 대체로, 주로
    • biological — 생물학적인
    • rather than — ~라기보다는
    • failing — 결점, 결함

    구문 분석

    · who struggled to ~ dawn : 주격 관계대명사절이 teenagers를 뒤에서 수식. 주어가 길어져 동사 were dismissed가 멀리 떨어져 있다. · be dismissed as ~ : 'dismiss A as B(A를 B라고 치부하다)'의 수동태. · suggests that ~ : that절이 suggest의 목적어. 여기서 suggest는 '제안하다'가 아니라 '시사하다'라서 that절에 should(가정법 현재)를 쓰지 않는다. · A rather than B : 'B라기보다 A' — biological과 a failing of character를 대비.

  2. During adolescence, the body's internal clock shifts by as much as two hours, so that a sixteen-year-old forced to wake at six is, in effect, being asked to function in the middle of his or her own night.

    해석 · 단어 · 구문 분석

    단어

    • adolescence — 청소년기, 사춘기

    • shift — 이동하다, 옮겨 가다
    • as much as — 무려 ~만큼이나
    • sixteen-year-old — 열여섯 살 아이
    • force — 억지로 ~하게 하다
    • wake — 잠에서 깨다, 일어나다

    구문 분석

  4. Yet the reform has proved surprisingly hard to sell, because a later start ripples through bus schedules, after-school sports, and the working hours of parents who rely on dropping their children off on the way to the office.

    해석 · 단어 · 구문 분석

    단어

    • sell — (생각을) 납득시키다, 설득하다
    • ripple through — (영향이) 파문처럼 ~에 번지다

  5. Critics also warn that teenagers may simply stay up later to compensate, although studies tracking students over several years have found that most of the extra time in the morning is genuinely spent asleep.

    해석 · 단어 · 구문 분석

    비판론자들은 또한 십대들이 그만큼 그냥 더 늦게까지 깨어 있을 수도 있다고 경고하지만, 여러 해에 걸쳐 학생들을 추적한 연구들은 아침에 늘어난 시간의 대부분이 실제로 잠자는 데 쓰인다는 것을 밝혀냈다.

    단어

    • critic — 비판론자, 비평가
    • warn — 경고하다
    • simply — 그저, 단순히
    • stay up — (자지 않고) 깨어 있다
    • compensate — 상쇄하다, 보충하다
    • although — ~이긴 하지만
    • track — 추적하다
    • several — 몇몇의, 여러
    • extra — 추가의, 여분의
    • genuinely — 진짜로, 실제로
    • asleep — 잠든

    구문 분석

    · warn that ~ : that절이 warn의 목적어. · to compensate : to부정사의 부사적 용법(목적) — '(늦게 일어나는 만큼을) 상쇄하려고'. · although ~ : 양보의 부사절. 주절의 우려를 뒤에서 반박하는 구조. · tracking students over several years : 현재분사구가 studies를 뒤에서 수식. · is spent asleep : spend의 수동태 + 보어 asleep(잠든 상태로 보내지다).
