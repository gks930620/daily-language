오늘의 문단

경제·노동 — 월급은 그대로 두고 하루를 뺀 '주 4일 근무제' 실험, 성공담은 정말 모든 일터에 통할까(논설)

Proponents of the four-day workweek point to a growing body of pilot programs in which companies cut hours by a fifth without trimming pay and, remarkably, reported little or no drop in output. Freed from the drudgery of a fifth day, employees in these trials said they slept better, took fewer sick days, and were far less inclined to quit, savings that partly offset the cost of the shorter schedule. Skeptics counter, however, that the results are skewed by self-selection: the firms that volunteered were largely white-collar businesses already convinced the idea would work, hardly a representative sample of the wider economy. For a hospital ward or a factory floor, where output is tied directly to the hours someone is physically present, compressing the week merely means hiring more staff or squeezing the same workload into four exhausting days. The more modest lesson of the experiments, then, may be not that Friday is dispensable but that much of the modern workday is padded with meetings and busywork that few would miss if they quietly disappeared.

conflate v. (서로 다른 것을) 혼동하다, 하나로 뭉뚱그리다

Critics argue that the study conflates working fewer hours with working more efficiently.
비판자들은 그 연구가 더 적게 일하는 것과 더 효율적으로 일하는 것을 뭉뚱그린다고 주장한다.

💡 con-(함께) + flate(불다) — 두 개를 한데 불어 넣어 하나로 만든다. 같은 뿌리: inflate(안으로 불다=부풀리다), deflate(바람을 빼다).
파생conflation(n.) 혼동, 융합
구분confuse(혼동하다) — confuse는 실수로 헷갈리는 것, conflate는 별개의 개념을 하나로 섞어 버리는 논리적 오류에 주로 쓴다
conflate A with B, conflate the two

outlier n. 이상치, 예외적인 존재

Analysts warned that the company's record profits might be an outlier rather than a trend.
분석가들은 그 회사의 기록적인 이익이 추세라기보다 예외적인 경우일 수 있다고 경고했다.

💡 out + lie(놓여 있다) + -er: 무리에서 '바깥에 떨어져 놓인 것'. 통계에서 평균을 왜곡하는 튀는 값 — '일반화하면 안 된다'는 논리에 자주 등장한다.
statistical outlier, an outlier rather than the norm
