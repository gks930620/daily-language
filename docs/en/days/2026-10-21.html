환경·과학 — 은하수를 지워 버린 밤의 불빛, 철새와 바다거북을 길 잃게 하는 '빛 공해'와 스위치 하나로 끝나는 오염이라는 역설(논설)

For almost all of human history, a star-filled sky was something people saw every clear night, yet the advent of cheap electric lighting has quietly obliterated that view, hiding the Milky Way from more than a third of humanity. Unlike smog or plastic waste, this kind of pollution leaves no visible residue, which is precisely why its ecological costs are so easily overlooked. Migrating birds that navigate by celestial cues are drawn toward brightly lit skyscrapers, where vast numbers die colliding with glass every year, while sea turtle hatchlings, instinctively crawling toward the brightest horizon, wander inland instead of toward the sea. Ironically, the switch from warm incandescent bulbs to energy-efficient LEDs has in some places made matters worse, because cities that saved money on electricity simply installed more lights, many of them emitting the bluish hue that most disrupts animals' internal clocks. Yet light pollution may be the easiest environmental problem to solve: unlike carbon dioxide, which lingers in the atmosphere for centuries, artificial light disappears the instant a switch is flipped.

trade-off n. (얻는 것과 잃는 것 사이의) 맞바꿈, 절충

There is always a trade-off between speed and accuracy.
속도와 정확성 사이에는 늘 하나를 얻으면 하나를 잃는 관계가 있다.

💡 trade off(교환해서 내주다)에서 온 명사 — '하나를 얻으려면 다른 하나를 내줘야 하는' 관계. 경제 기사에서 'between A and B'와 짝으로 거의 항상 나온다.
구분compromise(타협) — compromise는 사람 사이의 양보·합의, trade-off는 두 가치 사이의 구조적 맞바꿈
a trade-off between A and B, make a trade-off

subtle a. 미묘한, 은근한; 교묘한

The changes to the logo were subtle, but loyal customers noticed them.
로고의 변화는 미묘했지만 단골 고객들은 그것을 알아챘다.

💡 b는 묵음 — 발음은 [sʌ́tl] '서틀'. 명사형 subtlety도 b가 묵음이다. 'obvious(뻔한)'의 반대 자리에 놓으면 뜻이 잘 잡힌다.
파생subtlety(n.) 미묘함, 섬세함subtly(ad.) 미묘하게, 은근히
a subtle difference, subtle changes
