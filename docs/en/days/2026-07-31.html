보건·경제 — 초가공식품 규제와 설탕세를 둘러싼 논쟁(논설)

Ultra-processed foods, engineered in laboratories to be as palatable as possible, now account for more than half of the calories consumed in many wealthy countries. Public health officials attribute much of the rising prevalence of obesity and metabolic disease to these products, though the industry insists that the evidence remains inconclusive. Critics counter that such appeals to scientific uncertainty are a familiar delaying tactic, one perfected by tobacco companies decades ago, and that waiting for definitive proof would be an onerous standard no regulator could ever meet. Governments that have imposed a levy on sugary drinks report that manufacturers quietly reformulated their recipes rather than absorb the cost, a shift that reached consumers without requiring them to change their habits. Yet the backlash has been fierce: opponents denounce such measures as paternalistic and regressive, arguing that flat taxes on cheap staples fall hardest on the households least able to pay.

outweigh v. ~보다 더 크다, 능가하다

The long-term savings outweigh the initial cost of the equipment.
장기적인 절감액이 장비의 초기 비용보다 크다.

💡 동사 앞의 out-은 '~보다 더 잘/더 많이'를 뜻한다(outnumber 수적으로 앞서다, outperform 능가하다, outlive 더 오래 살다). 찬반을 저울질하는 논설문에서 결론을 짓는 핵심 동사다.
