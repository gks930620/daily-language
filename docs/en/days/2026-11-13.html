경제 — '선구매 후결제(BNPL)'의 급성장과 신용 기록에 잡히지 않는 '유령 부채' 논란(해설 기사)

"Buy now, pay later" services, which let shoppers split a purchase into a handful of interest-free installments at the checkout, have ballooned from a niche convenience into a mainstream form of credit in barely a decade. Their appeal is easy to grasp: no lengthy application, no hard credit check, and none of the stigma that clings to a maxed-out credit card. Yet regulators increasingly worry that this very ease is what makes the product hazardous, because many of these loans go unreported to credit bureaus, creating a pool of "phantom debt" that lenders cannot see. A borrower can thus juggle half a dozen plans from different providers at once, each individually manageable but collectively ruinous, and the warning signs surface only when payments start to bounce. Proponents counter that most users repay on time and that the late fees are modest compared with revolving card interest, but critics argue that averages obscure the plight of the most financially stretched households, who are precisely the ones the industry courts most aggressively.

grasp v./n. 이해하다, 파악하다; 꽉 쥐다

It took the new manager weeks to fully grasp the complexities of the budget.
새 매니저가 예산의 복잡한 구조를 완전히 파악하는 데 몇 주가 걸렸다.

💡 손으로 '꽉 움켜쥐다' → 개념을 '머리로 붙잡다' = 이해하다. grasp at straws(지푸라기라도 붙잡다)는 원뜻 그대로.
구분grip(꽉 쥠; 장악) — grip은 '쥐고 있는 상태·통제력'(lose one's grip=통제력을 잃다), grasp은 '붙잡는 동작·이해'에 무게가 있다
grasp the concept, a firm grasp of, beyond one's grasp

embezzle v. 횡령하다

The accountant was arrested for embezzling $2 million from client accounts.
그 회계사는 고객 계좌에서 200만 달러를 횡령한 혐의로 체포되었다.

💡 그냥 훔치는 steal과 달리, 관리하라고 '믿고 맡겨진' 돈을 빼돌린다는 배신이 핵심 — 그래서 주어는 대개 회계사·임원·공무원 같은 내부자다.
파생embezzlement(n.) 횡령embezzler(n.) 횡령범
구분misappropriate(유용하다) — 목적 외로 돈을 쓰는 것까지 포함하는 더 넓고 완곡한 법률 용어 — embezzle은 자기 주머니로 가져간 경우
embezzle funds, embezzlement charges
